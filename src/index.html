Series of Tubes: Australia. Run an Aussie ISP, one tube at a time

A free cartoon browser game about running an Australian ISP and hosting provider. Start with one data centre in a capital, buy IP transit, peer at internet exchanges, reach the Nearly Broadband Network's 121 points of interconnect over leased waves or your own dark fibre, rent an NNI at each POI and pay for every home's AVC, and rent racks to colocation and dedicated-server customers. Keep it all running while cyclones, backhoes, sharks, fat fingers, route leaks and DDoS attacks break things.
